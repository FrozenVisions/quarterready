Making Tax Digital: April 2027 checklist for sole traders & landlords

Source: GOV.UK. Not tax advice.

  1. Check qualifying income: gross self-employment + property income (before expenses) for 2025–26. Over £30,000 → MTD from 6 April 2027. Over £20,000 in 2026–27 → from April 2028.
  2. Sign up early on GOV.UK (HMRC urges this).
  3. Choose compatible software – HMRC's software finder lists free and paid options, including bridging software that works with spreadsheets.
  4. Keep digital records from day one: date, amount, category of every transaction.
  5. Diary the quarterly updates (HMRC's own example: a second quarterly update due 7 November for the 2026 cohort).
  6. You still file a final tax return for the year.
  7. Use a spreadsheet with formulas (no copy-paste) so the digital link holds.
