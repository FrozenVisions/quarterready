Do I need MTD if I earn under £30,000?

Short answer: not from April 2027. But if your qualifying income is over £20,000 in the 2026 to 2027 tax year, you will need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax from 6 April 2028 (GOV.UK).

It's gross income, not profit

Qualifying income is gross self-employment plus property income before expenses (GOV.UK). A sole trader with £28,000 sales and £4,000 rent has £32,000 qualifying income.

Under £20,000?

GOV.UK says the government will keep under review whether to require MTD for incomes below £30,000, and that people below the threshold can still sign up voluntarily. The £20,000 threshold from April 2028 is now in HMRC's guidance (GOV.UK). Volunteers don't get penalties for late quarterly updates (GOV.UK).

How many people

HMRC estimates 975,000 people with qualifying income between £20,000 and £30,000 will join in April 2028 (GOV.UK statistics).
