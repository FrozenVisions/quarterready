Can I use a spreadsheet for Making Tax Digital?

Short answer: yes, you can keep your records in a spreadsheet, but the figures must reach HMRC through compatible software. For spreadsheets that usually means bridging software that reads your sheet and sends the update. HMRC lists compatible products on GOV.UK (GOV.UK: choose software).

What the spreadsheet has to do

Your quarterly updates are totals for each income and expense category you used in your digital records. HMRC doesn't get individual receipts or invoices (GOV.UK). So your sheet needs every transaction recorded with a category, and running totals from 6 April.

Bridging software vs full accounting software

  • Bridging: you keep your spreadsheet, and the software sends the totals.
  • Record-keeping software: you move your records into an app.

Both are compatible routes. Check the GOV.UK finder for products that fit you, including free ones.

Tips

  • Use formulas for totals rather than retyping numbers.
  • Use the same expense categories as Self Assessment.
  • Diary the 7th-of-the-month deadlines.
