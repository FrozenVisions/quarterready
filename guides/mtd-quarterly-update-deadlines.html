MTD for Income Tax: quarterly update deadlines

Short answer: quarterly updates are due by 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May (the following tax year), whether you use standard or calendar update periods (GOV.UK).

Standard update periods (tax year 6 April to 5 April)

PeriodDeadline
6 April to 5 July7 August
6 April to 5 October7 November
6 April to 5 January7 February
6 April to 5 April7 May (following tax year)

Calendar update periods (accounts 1 April to 31 March)

PeriodDeadline
1 April to 30 June7 August
1 April to 30 September7 November
1 April to 31 December7 February
1 April to 31 March7 May (following tax year)

Each update is cumulative: it covers the tax year so far, not just the latest three months. You must still send an update if you had no income or expenses in the period. You can't change your period type for the rest of the year once you've sent an update (GOV.UK).

Penalties

HMRC will not apply penalty points for late quarterly updates in the 2026 to 2027 tax year. After that, each late update gets a point, and 4 points means a £200 penalty. Late tax returns still get points (GOV.UK).
