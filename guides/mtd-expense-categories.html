Making Tax Digital expense categories explained

Short answer: Making Tax Digital for Income Tax uses the same income and expense categories as Self Assessment (GOV.UK). The exact category list is set out in HMRC's quarterly update direction.

Allowable expenses you can claim

GOV.UK lists these types of allowable business costs for sole traders (GOV.UK):

  • Office costs, e.g. stationery or phone bills
  • Travel costs, e.g. fuel, parking, train or bus fares
  • Clothing expenses, e.g. uniforms
  • Staff costs, e.g. salaries or subcontractor costs
  • Things you buy to sell on, e.g. stock or raw materials
  • Financial costs, e.g. insurance or bank charges
  • Business premises costs, e.g. heating, lighting, business rates
  • Advertising or marketing, e.g. website costs
  • Subscriptions to trade or professional journals
  • Training courses related to your business

Mixed business and personal costs

Only the business part counts. GOV.UK's example: of £200 phone bills, £70 business use means you claim £70 (GOV.UK).

Things to watch

  • You can't claim expenses if you use the £1,000 trading allowance.
  • Equipment, machinery and vehicles may be capital allowances under traditional accounting.
  • Simplified flat-rate expenses exist for vehicles and working from home (GOV.UK).
